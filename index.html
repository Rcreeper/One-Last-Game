EASY GO FISH RULES

HOW TO WIN IN 4 SIMPLE STEPS

  • 1. SET STAKES & DEAL: Choose your match wager and receive 5 cards (the Wolf Dealer also gets 5).
  • 2. ASK FOR A RANK: Pick any rank already in your hand (e.g., 7s or Kings) and ask the Wolf Dealer for it.
  • 3. TAKE CARDS OR "GO FISH": If the Dealer has that rank, you take all of them and go again! If not, you draw 1 card from The Pond.
  • 4. COLLECT BOOKS (4 OF A KIND): Whenever you get all 4 cards of the same rank, they automatically score as a Book. Most Books when all 13 are completed wins the pot!
